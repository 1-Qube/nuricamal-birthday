# NURICAMAL — The World in Her Colors
### Түстер Әлемі · Мир в Её Красках

An interactive digital art exhibition celebrating Nuricamal's 12th birthday.  
*A little girl. An infinite canvas. A world of colors.*

🎨 **Live Exhibition:** [https://1-qube.github.io/nuricamal-birthday/](https://1-qube.github.io/nuricamal-birthday/)

---

## The Concept
Designed not as a conventional birthday template, but as a high-end contemporary art gallery monograph. The site guides visitors through five chapters:
1. **The Canvas (Opening)** — Cover monograph with fluid watercolor bleeding and reactive paint-trail cursor.
2. **A World of Colors** — Editorial pigment studies: Cobalt Blue, Vermilion Red, Butter Yellow, Viridian Sage, and Sumi Bone Ink.
3. **The Interactive Canvas** — Full digital art studio with 5 authentic textures (Watercolor Wash, Heavy Gouache, Sketch Charcoal, Pigment Splatter, Linen Eraser), stroke controls, undo history, and export.
4. **An Unfinished Collection (The Gallery)** — Four photographs of Nuricamal (sweets, roses, the garden, the laughing hour), hung with museum placards. Click any frame to enlarge.
5. **The Birthday Reveal** — Secret dedication triggering a full-screen celebration with artisanal confetti physics and interactive color bursts.
6. **Epilogue** — Minimalist closing manifesto: *"The world is your canvas. Keep creating, Nuricamal."*

## Features
- **Trilingual Experience**: Seamless instant switching between **Kazakh (KZ)**, **English (EN)**, and **Russian (RU)**.
- **Fine-Art Typography**: Pairing *Syne* & *Montserrat* with *Fraunces* & *Playfair Display* and *Space Mono* & *JetBrains Mono*.
- **Tactile Aesthetics**: Custom procedural fine-art paper grain and traditional painting pigments.
- **100% Vanilla Code**: Pure HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript. Exactly three core files, zero npm packages, zero external frameworks.
